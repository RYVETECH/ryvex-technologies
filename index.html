ARCR-UGV

Autonomous Range Cartridge Retriever — Tracked UGV

Designed to solve critical military and shooting range bottlenecks. After firing drills, thousands of high-value brass casings (9mm, 5.56mm, 7.62mm) litter hot grounds, presenting both extreme manual labor costs and dangerous hazards from unexploded rounds. ARCR-UGV traverses rugged terrains, scoops spent cartridges, separates debris through a calibrated mechanical rotary trommel, and uses edge AI to safely flag live ammunition and UXO hazards before chambering.

▸ Mobility: High-torque tracked tank chassis with heavy mud treads
▸ Sorting Drum: 3-stage calibrated rotary spiral trommel sieve
▸ Sensor Coil: Sub-surface pulsed induction metal detector
▸ Hazard AI: Edge vision classifier distinguishing spent brass from live UXO

VIGIL-X4

Multi-Domain Tactical Command Carrier Mothership

The flagship innovation selected among the Top 100 out of 1,100 national teams at IIT Roorkee National Techathon 3.0. VIGIL-X4 is an all-terrain tactical mothership built on an asynchronous dual-microcontroller architecture. The Master ESP32 governs heavy-duty tank locomotion via dual 43A BTS7960 H-bridges, long-range LoRa 433MHz telemetry, and FlySky 2.4GHz control. The Slave ESP32 coordinates the rear motorized tailgate garage, launching a micro-recon scout car, flying an overhead surveillance quadcopter from its roof pad, and operating a 5-DOF high-torque robotic manipulator arm.

▸ Architecture: Master-Slave Dual ESP32 with zero-packet-drop ESP-NOW
▸ Aerial Sub-Unit: Top-deck motorized quadcopter launch and recovery station
▸ Ground Sub-Unit: Internal rear garage deploying autonomous scout mini-rover
▸ Kinematics: 5-DOF robotic arm with high-torque metal gear servos
▸ Telemetry & Video: Tapo IP 360° pan-tilt camera + ESP32-CAM + LoRa 433MHz
▸ Atmospheric Suite: Real-time MQ-135 hazardous gas and DHT11 thermal sensors

EcoGuardian

Autonomous 4-Way Intelligent Segregation Ecosystem

Awarded First Position at the State Science Fair (Prantiya Vigyan Mela 2026) and recognized by NITI Aayog's Atal Community Innovation Centre (ACIC MIET). EcoGuardian tackles the global crisis of unsorted waste at the point of disposal. Utilizing a multi-sensor fusion pipeline (inductive proximity, capacitive permittivity, and soil moisture probes), the unit autonomously classifies waste into Metal, Plastic, Wet, and Dry fractions, indexing a precision rotary tray diverter while reporting fill levels to the "Thursday" AI core.

▸ Segregation: 4 distinct compartments (Metallic, Plastic, Wet Bio, Dry Fibrous)
▸ Sorting Mechanism: NEMA stepper-driven multi-angle rotary index table
▸ Sensor Fusion: Inductive + Capacitive + Soil Resistivity + Ultrasonic
▸ AI Companion: "Thursday" virtual assistant running voice telemetry

ENGINEERING ITERATION TRAJECTORY

The Robotics Evolution Triptych

Real engineering is forged through continuous iteration. Observe how Raman Luthra evolved early breadboard prototypes into high-precision autonomous rovers.

01 Generation 1

The Proof-of-Concept Flap

Constructed on a custom cardboard and acrylic bench frame. Validated fundamental wet vs dry classification using an Arduino Uno, basic moisture probes, and a single micro-servo dual-direction flap diverter.

  • Dual-category binary diversion (Wet / Dry)
  • Arduino Uno core running C++ finite state loop
  • Hand-wired perfboard and external 9V rail
  • Proof of fundamental sensor feasibility
02 Generation 2

The Rotary Chamber Sorter

Expanded to an industrial 4-compartment segregation unit. Integrated inductive metal detection, capacitive dielectric sensing, and a precision rotating circular tray indexing four separate collection bins.

  • 4-category sorting (Metal, Plastic, Wet, Dry)
  • Stepper motor with optical limit home switch
  • Ultrasonic sensors tracking real-time bin capacity
  • Awarded State 1st Position at Science Fair
03 Generation 3

The Autonomous Rover & AI

Transformed into an autonomous mobile field unit. Mounted onto a rugged tracked chassis with dual-ESP32 ESP-NOW networking, integrated "Thursday" AI core, cloud telemetry, and long-range remote dispatch.

THE MANIFESTO

Message from the Founder

Why a 16-year-old student is building defense and environmental robotics from Meerut to the world.

"True engineering is not born in multimillion-dollar corporate test labs—it is forged late at night on cluttered workbenches, fueled by soldering smoke, burned-out resistors, and a stubborn refusal to accept limits."

My journey began not with grants or fancy facilities, but with taking apart broken toys and salvaged circuit boards on my desk in Meerut. At an age when most are content with classroom textbooks, I felt an urgent calling: why should our armed forces and environmental rangers rely on foreign technology when we have the intellect and grit to engineer superior solutions right here?

Every millimeter of RYVEX hardware—whether it is the Trommel Sieve on the ARCR-UGV collecting unexploded ammunition, or the dual-ESP32 mothership architecture on VIGIL-X4 chosen for the IIT Roorkee Techathon 3.0 Finals—has been designed, coded, soldered, and debugged with one core conviction: India will lead the next century of autonomous defense and ecological robotics.

RYVEX Technologies is my pledge to that vision. We do not build toys; we build mission-ready systems.

Raman Luthra Founder & Chief Robotics Architect | RYVEX Technologies Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India // Class of 2026
